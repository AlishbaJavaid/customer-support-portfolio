01 · About

Listen, reproduce, explain, follow up

I'm a software QA engineer with over five years of experience testing web and mobile applications in Agile teams. Most of my work has been finding what is broken or confusing for users, reproducing it, and explaining it clearly so it gets fixed. That is also the core of good support: listen carefully, reproduce the problem, communicate clearly, and follow up until it is resolved.

Most recently, as Senior QA Engineer at CyberMart, I tested an eCommerce platform from the buyer's and seller's point of view, and helped reduce user-reported bugs by 30% and improve issue resolution time by 20%. I'm now looking to move into customer and technical support roles, where I can use my troubleshooting, documentation, and communication skills to help users directly.

05 · Work Samples

How I write to customers and teams

Support writing samples. These are examples I wrote, not messages from real customers.

SAMPLE · CUSTOMER REPLY

Verification code not arriving

Hi Sara,

Thanks for letting us know, and sorry for the trouble. Let's get you signed in.

First, please check your spam or junk folder for an email from us, and make sure your email address is spelled correctly. If it isn't there, wait two minutes and select "Resend code" once. If the code still doesn't arrive, reply with the email address you used and roughly what time you tried, and I'll look into it and update you today.

Best regards,
Alishba

SAMPLE · REPLY TEMPLATE

Delayed order

Hi Daniel,

I'm sorry your order is late. I know that's frustrating when you're waiting on it. I've checked order #1042: it shows as shipped, and the tracking link has the latest update: [tracking link].

If the tracking hasn't changed by [date], reply to this message and I'll raise it with our shipping team and keep you updated until it's sorted.

Thank you for your patience.
Alishba

SAMPLE · HELP ARTICLE

Didn't get your verification code?

If your code hasn't arrived, try these steps in order:

  1. Check your spam or junk folder.
  2. Make sure the email address you entered is spelled correctly.
  3. Wait two minutes, then select "Resend code" once.
  4. Still nothing? Contact support with the email address you used and the time you tried.
SAMPLE · ESCALATION

Handing an issue to engineering

Title: Verification email not sent during signup

Steps: Open signup, enter a valid new email, select "Send OTP", wait 5 minutes.

Expected: An email with the code arrives within about a minute.

Actual: The screen says "OTP sent", but no email arrives.

Impact: New users can't finish signing up.

Suggested priority: High
